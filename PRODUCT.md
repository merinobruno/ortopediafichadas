# Product

## Platform
web
## Stack
Approved architecture: TypeScript modular application; React and Express chosen for local implementation.
## Users
HR staff managing employees across multiple sites; employees use WhatsApp native location sharing.
## Product Purpose
Record every visit, keep exceptions visible, and eliminate manual attendance transcription.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Per-site entries and exits, multiple daily visits, geofences, authorized phone numbers, audit corrections. A new-site entry marks the prior open visit exit_unknown and alerts HR.
## Brand Commitments
Carahue artwork: petroleum/emerald, orange, light backgrounds. Argentine Spanish interface.
## Evidence on Hand
Approved attendance design; supplied diseños.zip artwork. All demonstration people and records are synthetic.
## Open decisions
Production hosting, Meta credentials, PostgreSQL deployment and extended reference integrations.
